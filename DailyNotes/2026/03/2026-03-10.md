### Distributed Systems
#### Chapter 1: Introduction
- Computing system development is growing in a fast pace due to 2 main reasons 
  - More powerful computing power
  - More and more networked machines with more powerful bandwidth
- Distributed system is a collections of connected systems where processes and resources are spreaded across machines
- Design goals for distributed systems:
  - System openness
  - Resource sharing
  - System security
- Design goals can vary depending on the types of desired systems
